PROJECT ABSTRACT 
Modern computational intelligence systems in quantitative finance face severe real-world challenges stemming 
from non-stationary market regimes, low signal-to-noise ratios, and the divergence between statistical 
classification loss and portfolio equity dynamics. This minor specialization capstone project presents a 
comprehensive, reproducible Two-Tier Computational Intelligence (CI) system evaluated through a retrospective 6-
year walk-forward campaign across three large-cap National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) equities: Reliance 
Industries (RELIANCE.NS), Tata Consultancy Services (TCS.NS), and Infosys (INFY.NS) over 2020–2025 (1,487 trading 
days per asset). 
In Tier 1, a LightGBM gradient-boosted decision tree is trained on 20 technical momentum, trend, volatility, and 
volume indicators to forecast 3-day forward directional returns with market volatility regime detection. In Tier 2, 
the Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II (NSGA-II) evolves continuous trading rule chromosomes 
(entry/exit probability hurdles, dynamic ATR stop-loss and take-profit multipliers, trailing stops, and volatility-
conditioned position sizing) to simultaneously optimize Annualized Sharpe Ratio and Maximum Drawdown. A 
custom vectorized simulator enforces strict next-bar Open execution, commissions and slippage (0.05% 
commission + 0.05% slippage per side, approximately 10 bps per side, 20 bps round-trip; 40 bps doubled 
sensitivity), opening gaps, conservative intrabar bracket conflict resolution, horizon purging, and scheduled fold-
end liquidation barring final-day entries. 
The empirical results provide key scientific insights: LightGBM achieves an average out-of-sample directional AUC 
of 0.5324 and test accuracy of 52.68% (vs training-class base rate 52.66%), confirming that machine learning offers 
modest statistical discrimination in liquid equity markets. The Full Hybrid strategy dampens annual maximum 
drawdown in 17 out of 18 folds (94.4%). However, by remaining defensively in cash during multi-month 
expansions, the Hybrid underperforms Buy & Hold in 13 out of 18 folds (72.2%), compounding 6-year seed-median 
returns of -0.35% (RELIANCE.NS), +25.60% (TCS.NS), and +9.10% (INFY.NS), compared to Buy & Hold compound 
growth of +128.74%, +65.13%, and +152.78%, respectively. An evolutionary ablation without ML signals yields 
comparable or higher returns. These findings document the profound opportunity costs of defensive capital 
allocation and establish the necessity of reproducible, causal simulation in financial AI.

CHAPTER 1 
Introduction & Problem Definition 
1.1 Background & Motivation 
Financial market forecasting represents one of the most intellectually challenging domains in computational 
intelligence. Unlike computer vision or speech recognition where underlying data-generating distributions remain 
stationary over time, financial time-series are characterized by time-varying dynamics, extreme noise, structural 
regime shifts, and reflexive feedback loops [1], [5]. A common pitfall in applied computer science projects is 
treating stock trading as a pure supervised regression or classification problem. In practice, a predictive model 
achieving 55% directional accuracy will rapidly deplete portfolio equity if position sizing, trade timing, stop-loss 
mechanics, and transaction friction are unmanaged [3]. 
Conversely, pure rule-based algorithmic trading strategies rely on static technical indicators that suffer 
catastrophic drawdowns during macroeconomic shocks. What is required is a dual architecture: statistical machine 
learning to extract subtle probabilistic edges from high-dimensional indicators, coupled with multi-objective 
metaheuristics to evolve dynamic risk-management policies that directly optimize the portfolio equity trajectory. 
1.2 Objectives of the Capstone Study 
This project pursues four core engineering and scientific objectives: (1) Build an auditable feature engineering 
pipeline extracting 20 momentum, trend, volatility, and volume indicators alongside an expanding-window 
volatility regime detector; (2) Implement a LightGBM gradient-boosted classifier to estimate forward 3-day 
directional probabilities with validation early stopping; (3) Implement an NSGA-II multi-objective genetic algorithm 
that evolves 7-dimensional real-valued chromosomes optimizing Annualized Sharpe Ratio and Maximum 
Drawdown; (4) Execute a rigorous retrospective walk-forward evaluation across six annual folds (2020–2025) on 
three NSE equities (RELIANCE.NS, TCS.NS, INFY.NS) with complete offline reproducibility, transaction costs, and 
horizon purging. 
CHAPTER 2 
Literature Review & Theoretical Foundations 
2.1 Market Efficiency & Technical Predictability 
The theoretical foundation of market predictability is rooted in Eugene Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH) 
[6]. Under the weak-form EMH, current asset prices fully reflect all information contained in historical trading data. 
Consequently, technical analysis rules cannot consistently generate risk-adjusted excess returns (alpha) net of 
transaction costs. In modern econometric literature, Halbert White [5] demonstrated that apparent historical 
outperformance is frequently an artifact of data snooping bias: when researchers evaluate thousands of rule 
combinations, the best-performing rule often reflects luck rather than predictive skill. Bailey and López de Prado 
[4] formalized this phenomenon through the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR), which statistically adjusts observed 
performance for selection bias and non-normal return distributions. 
2.2 Purging, Embargoing & Causal Simulation Integrity 
In Advances in Financial Machine Learning, Marcos López de Prado [3] identified temporal leakage as the primary 
reason why financial machine learning models collapse when deployed out-of-sample. When multi-day forward 
targets are assigned to daily bars, consecutive samples share overlapping information. Traditional random k-fold

cross-validation leaks forward-looking information into the training set. To enforce causal integrity, chronological 
train/validation/test splits must incorporate horizon purging: dropping trailing samples equal to the forward label 
horizon (H=3) prior to split boundaries. 
2.3 Evolutionary Multi-Objective Optimization & Gradient Boosting 
The Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II (NSGA-II) developed by Deb et al. [1] represents a benchmark 
standard in multi-objective evolutionary computation. NSGA-II addresses the computational bottlenecks of early 
genetic algorithms through fast non-dominated sorting, elitist parent-offspring combining, and a parameter-less 
crowding distance operator that preserves population diversity along the Pareto front. In tabular machine learning, 
Ke et al. [2] introduced LightGBM, which leverages Gradient-based One-Side Sampling (GOSS) and Exclusive 
Feature Bundling (EFB) to deliver rapid, memory-efficient tree boosting, making it an ideal candidate for low-
latency financial signal generation.

The system decouples directional prediction from risk-constrained decision execution across two computational 
tiers: Tier 1 functions as a statistical feature extractor and probabilistic classifier, producing conditional directional 
probabilities p_hat_t = P(y_t = 1 | x_t). Tier 2 acts as a metaheuristic policy engine, searching continuous trading 
rule parameters to balance risk and return directly on portfolio equity.

3.3 Bi-Objective Optimization Formulation & Operators 
NSGA-II evaluates each candidate chromosome across the 2-year Development partition under two conflicting 
objective functions: (1) Maximize Annualized Sharpe Ratio f_1 = (mu_r / sigma_r) * sqrt(252) (assuming a 0.0% 
risk-free rate); (2) Minimize Maximum Drawdown f_2 = max_t ((Peak_t - Equity_t) / Peak_t). A candidate solution a 
Pareto-dominates solution b (a dominates b) if f_1(a) >= f_1(b) and f_2(a) <= f_2(b), with at least one strict 
inequality. 
Evolutionary search uses population size N=30 across G=10 generations. Variation operators comprise Simulated 
Binary Crossover (SBX, crossover probability pc=0.85, distribution index eta_c=15) and Polynomial Mutation (per -
gene mutation probability pm = 1/7, distribution index eta_m=20). To penalize inactivity during development, 
candidate rules generating fewer than 5 completed trades receive a fitness penalty: Sharpe is decremented by 2.0 
(Sharpe -= 2.0) and Maximum Drawdown is floored at 0.5 (MaxDD = max(MaxDD, 0.5)). Following search, the 
solution with the highest penalized development Sharpe ratio is selected. If no candidate completes >= 5 trades, 
the system defaults to 100% Cash during test evaluation. 
CHAPTER 4 
Data Engineering & Walk-Forward Protocol 
4.1 Data Acquisition & Snapshot Manifest 
To guarantee complete offline reproducibility, historical daily OHLCV series for Reliance Industries (RELIANCE.NS), 
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS.NS), and Infosys (INFY.NS) were cached in immutable Apache Parquet format under 
reports/experiments/inputs/. Cryptographic SHA-256 hashes for all snapshots are tracked in manifest.json. 
Historical prices reflect Yahoo Finance dividend- and split-adjusted series (auto_adjust=True), ensuring corporate 
actions do not induce artificial price discontinuities.

Averages (dist_sma_10, dist_sma_50, dist_sma_200); (2) Momentum Indicators: MACD ratio (macd / close), MACD

4.3 Chronological Partitions & Horizon Purging 
For each test year Y in [2020, 2025], data partitions are strictly partitioned: Training [Y-8, Y-4] (5 years, trailing 3 
bars purged); Validation [Y-3] (1 year, trailing 3 bars purged); Development [Y-2, Y-1] (2 years, NSGA-II search, 
trailing 3 bars purged); and Test [Y] (1 year, out-of-sample). Purging the trailing 3 bars of training and validation 
prevents the 3-day forward target from overlapping into adjacent out-of-sample sets. On the test partition, the 
final 3 bars are masked from classification scoring because their forward targets resolve after the test window. 
CHAPTER 5 
High-Performance Vectorized Simulator Mechanics 
5.1 Next-Bar Execution & Causal Timing 
The backtest engine executes strictly on the Open price following signal generation: if LightGBM probability 
p_hat_t >= p_entry at bar t Close, a long position is queued and filled at bar t+1 Open at price P_entry = 
Open_{t+1} * (1 + slip). Same-bar fill prices are prohibited. The first trade on test bar 0 utilizes causal prefix context 
(probability and regime state) evaluated at the final session of the Development partition (t=-1), ensuring seamless 
multi-year continuity. 
5.2 Intrabar Bracket Ordering & Friction Policies 
To avoid optimistic fill bias, the simulator enforces strict intraday priority rules: (1) Stop-Loss Priority: If the session 
Low touches the stop-loss and the session High touches the take-profit on the same day, the stop-loss is 
prioritized; (2) Opening Gaps: Opening gap downs below the stop price execute at the open price with slippage 
penalty; (3) Zero-Volume Prohibition: Zero-volume bars cannot execute trades; (4) Scheduled Fold-End Liquidation: 
Prohibits new position entries on the final tradable session (i < last_tradable_idx) and liquidates open positions at 
final session Close; (5) Transaction Costs: Standard friction is 0.05% commission and 0.05% slippage per side 
(approximately 10 bps per side, 20 bps round-trip); sensitivity tests double friction to 0.10% per side 
(approximately 40 bps round-trip).

6.4 Ablation and Exposure-Matched Diagnostic 
When NSGA-II evolved rules with a constant probability (p=0.60, omitting ML features), the Ablation achieved 6-
year stitched returns of +42.42% for RELIANCE.NS, +47.37% for TCS.NS, and +18.69% for INFY.NS—exceeding the 
Full Hybrid on all assets. Furthermore, an Exposure-Matched Buy & Hold diagnostic blending passive holding with 
cash based on the Hybrid's development exposure fraction (w_dev) outperformed the Full Hybrid in 29 of 54 runs 
(53.7%), while the Hybrid outperformed in 22 runs (40.7%), with 3 ties (RELIANCE 2020 cash fallbacks). Exposure-
Matched Buy & Hold exhibited lower maximum drawdown than the Full Hybrid in 43 of 54 runs (79.6%). This 
diagnostic assumes daily frictionless rebalancing and 0% cash return. This indicates that defensive metaheuristic 
rules provide robust risk containment, but the ML signal added no measurable excess return over technical 
filtering.

to +19.61% (-5.99 pp), and INFY.NS Seed 44 declined from +27.88% to -7.97% (-35.85 pp) (Table 6.4). Slippage 
operates non-linearly: higher entry prices elevate absolute ATR stop prices, causing intrabar stops to trigger on 
sessions that would have otherwise remained open.

CHAPTER 7 
Automated Verification, Unit Testing & Reproducibility 
7.1 Regression Test Suite 
The project includes an automated pytest suite covering Numba simulator execution timing, gap handling, stop-
loss priority, chronological split purging, real-fold offline reproducibility, trailing zero-volume liquidation, and final-
day entry prohibition. All tests pass offline in ~8 seconds with zero network calls. 
7.2 Campaign-Wide Automated Reproducibility Audit 
A standalone verification script (scripts/audit_campaign_reproducibility.py) re-runs all 18 annual folds across all 3 
seeds with monkeypatched yfinance download blockers, asserting that chosen chromosome genes match saved 
artifacts within 10^-12 and equity curves match within 10^-7. The audit evaluated 306 assertions with zero 
mismatches, confirming complete bit-level code reproducibility. Furthermore, scripts/verify_numerical_claims.py 
programmatically asserts all 47 quantitative claims reported across this capstone report against the underlying 
experiment CSV and JSON files with 100% pass rate.

Methodological Limitations & Research Boundaries 
The empirical conclusions are bounded by four key constraints: (1) Single-Asset Isolated Evaluation: Strategies are 
evaluated on individual stocks; cross-asset portfolio diversification and risk parity are not modeled; (2) Multi-Year 
vs Annual Drawdown Asymmetry: Lower annual drawdowns in 17 of 18 folds do not guarantee lower multi-year

adjusted OHLC series from Yahoo Finance are used rather than raw exchange order books; (5) Retrospective 
Protocol Corrections: The walk-forward protocol was refined through versioned retrospective audit corrections 
(v1.1.0 in protocol_correction.json). 
CHAPTER 9 
Conclusion & Future Directions 
This capstone project engineered and validated a Two-Tier Computational Intelligence trading system combining 
LightGBM and NSGA-II. Through strict walk-forward evaluation on three NSE equities over 2020–2025, the 
research established that while evolutionary rules successfully dampen annual drawdowns in 94.4% of folds, 
defensive cash-holding imposes heavy opportunity costs during secular bull regimes, resulting in lower multi-year

superior returns. Future research will explore cross-asset portfolio allocation, macroeconomic conditioning, and
